Catching card testing

Fraudsters test stolen cards with tiny charges. A simple rule flags every charge of $1.00 or less; the data's labels say which charges really were tests. Precision is the share of flagged charges that were tests; recall is the share of tests the rule flagged. It never raises a false alarm, but misses the probes above $1. (Across all categories.)
